Material: HW22, his sheet · Lesson Games “LP23 Spelling” — iPad

0A · HW22 check (5 min, 16:30–16:35)

Rules. Take the sheet. Say: “Show me your best sentence first.” Then go through Part 1 together; he reads each word aloud before you mark it.

Key — HW22 (from HW22’s key page)

Pt1: 1 night · 2 light · 3 kite · 4 bike · 5 right · 6 time

Pt2: 1 The kite is high. · 2 Turn on the light. · 3 I ride my bike at night.

Pt3 (picture match): moon → night · lamp → light · clock → time

Watch: “nite”, “lite” (spells the sound, not the pattern). Point at igh on the card; don’t say the letters.

Done when: every item checked and he has fixed his own mistakes in pencil.

0B · Spelling warm-up (5 min, 16:35–16:40)

Open Lesson Games “LP23 Spelling” and hand him the iPad. Feedback after each word is on: let the app tell him.

Say each word once, then in the sentence: “night — I sleep at night — night.”

  • ⚠ TRAP (night)
  • Spring: “night”, said slowly, no picture.
  • Right: night
  • Expect: nite
  • Repair: “What makes the long i in this family? Look at light.” Wait.
  • After a miss: write night and light one under the other; he circles igh in both.

Key

night · light · dream · boat · coat · snow · slow · goat

Done when: eight words typed; score on the screen.

SEGMENT 1 — NEW SOUND: OA / OW (~15 min, 16:40–16:55)

Material: WS23 p.1–2 · Lesson Games “LP23 Phonics Sort” — iPad

1A · Present oa and ow (8 min, 16:40–16:48)

Build. Hold up the boat card. Say: “Boat. Listen: b — oa — t. Two letters, one sound.” Write oa big on the whiteboard.

Then the snow card: “Snow. s — n — ow. Same sound, different letters.” Write ow beside it.

Rule to give him, word for word: “oa is usually in the middle of a word. ow is usually at the end.” Show: boat, coat, goat / snow, slow, grow.

Ask: “Where is the sound in road?” → middle → oa. “Where is it in show?” → end → ow.

  • ⚠ TRAP (cold)
  • Spring: “How do you spell bow, like a ribbon?”
  • Right: bow
  • Also right: b-o-w (said as letters)
  • Expect: boa
  • Repair: “Is the sound in the middle or at the end?” Wait.
  • After a miss: “End of the word: ow.” Write bow under snow.

Done when: he can tell you oa-or-ow for road, show, toast, blow without help.

1B · Phonics sort (7 min, 16:48–16:55)

Open Lesson Games “LP23 Phonics Sort”. He says each word aloud before he sorts it.

Key

oa: boat, coat, goat, road · ow: snow, slow, grow, show · igh: night, light, high

Done when: every word sorted; he has said each one aloud.

SEGMENT 2 — READER: GOAT BOAT (~12 min, 16:55–17:07)

Material: Goat Boat reader p.6–9 · WS23 p.2

2A · Read p.6–9 (7 min, 16:55–17:02)

Rules. He reads; you point. Say: “Find every oa or ow word on this page first. Tap them.” Then he reads the page aloud.

Stumbles to expect: floated, tomorrow, coast. Let him try twice, then say the word once and move on.

2B · Reading questions (5 min, 17:02–17:07)

Rules. Ask, he answers in a full sentence. Say: “Answer with the whole sentence, like the book.”

Reading key

1 Where did the goat go? → The goat went on the boat.
2 What did the goat wear? → The goat wore a yellow coat.
3 Why did the boat go slow? → Because the snow was deep / the road was white with snow.

Accept one-word answers only on question 3, then model the full sentence.

Done when: three questions answered, at least two in full sentences.

SEGMENT 3 — GAMES AND CLOSE (~11 min, 17:07–17:18)

3A · Rhyme pairs (5 min, 17:07–17:12)

Open Lesson Games “LP23 Rhyme Pairs”. He turns two cards; if they rhyme he says both words aloud before he keeps them.

Done when: all pairs found.

3B · Snakes & Ladders (4 min, 17:12–17:16)

3C · Homework HW23 (2 min, 17:16–17:18)

Done when: HW23 in his bag; he can tell you what Part 2 is.
