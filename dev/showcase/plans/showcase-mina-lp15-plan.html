SEGMENT 1 — GRAMMAR: DID / DIDN’T (~18 min, 19:10–19:28)

Material: textbook p.58 grammar box · Lesson Games “Past Verbs Type It” — her screen

1A · The grammar box (8 min, 19:10–19:18)

Present. Share p.58. Say: “Look at the verbs after did. What do you notice?” Let her find it: the verb goes back to its base form.

Write three lines and have her read them aloud:

She went to the museum. → Did she go to the museum? → She didn’t go to the museum.

He bought a cake. → Did he buy a cake? → He didn’t buy a cake.

They caught the bus. → Did they catch the bus? → They didn’t catch the bus.

Rule, word for word: “did carries the past. The verb after it doesn’t.”

  • ⚠ TRAP (did + past)
  • Spring: “Make it a question: She bought a new bag.”
  • Right: “Did she buy a new bag?”
  • Expect: “Did she bought a new bag?”
  • Repair: point at did. “did already says past. So the verb is…?” Wait.
