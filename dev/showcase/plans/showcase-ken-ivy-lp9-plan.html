SEGMENT 1 — GRAMMAR: WHAT IS IT DOING? (~14 min, 15:08–15:22)

Material: zoo picture (WS9 p.1) · Lesson Games “Animal Match Up” — iPad

1A · Questions and answers (8 min, 15:08–15:16)

Present. Point at the giraffe on WS9. Say: “What is the giraffe doing?” Model the answer: “It’s eating leaves.”

Write the pattern: What is the ___ doing? → It’s ___ing. / What are the ___ doing? → They’re ___ing.

Ken asks Ivy about one animal, then Ivy asks Ken. Three each.

  • ⚠ TRAP (be)
  • Spring: Ken, “What is the dolphin doing?”
  • Right: “It’s swimming.”
  • Also right: “It is jumping.”
  • Expect: “It swimming.”
  • Repair: tap the board where It’s is. Wait.
  • After a miss: both say the full sentence together, then Ken alone.
  • ⚠ TRAP (plural)
  • Spring: Ivy, “What are the penguins doing?”
  • Right: “They’re walking.”
  • Expect: “It’s walking.”
  • Repair: “One penguin or lots of penguins?” Wait.

1B · Animal Match Up (6 min, 15:16–15:22)

Open Lesson Games “Animal Match Up”. They take turns; each match is read aloud as a sentence: “The kangaroo is jumping.”

Done when: every pair matched.

SEGMENT 2 — READING: AT THE ZOO (~12 min, 15:22–15:34)

2A · Read p.12 (6 min, 15:22–15:28)

2B · Zoo Spot the Error (6 min, 15:28–15:34)

Open Lesson Games “Zoo Spot the Error”. Whoever spots the mistake says the fixed sentence; the other one types it.

Key
