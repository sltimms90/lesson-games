BEFORE CLASS

Laptop — Lesson Games: “Second Conditional Auction” · “If I Were Builder” · “Linkers Flash Cards”

SEGMENT 0 — WARM-UP (~8 min, 20:00–20:08)

0A · Who / which / whose (8 min, 20:00–20:08)

Rules. Say: “Finish my sentence: That’s the girl whose…” Three each way.

Key

whose + noun (whose bag) · who for people · which for things

SEGMENT 1 — GRAMMAR: SECOND CONDITIONAL (~20 min, 20:08–20:28)

Material: Lesson Games “Second Conditional Auction”, “If I Were Builder” — her screen

1A · Present (8 min, 20:08–20:16)

Present. Type: “If I had more time, I would read more.” Ask: “Do I have more time?” → no. “So is this real or imagined?” → imagined.

Rule, word for word: “If + past, would + base verb. It’s about now, but not real.”

  • ⚠ TRAP (would in both halves)
  • Spring: “Finish it: If schools banned phones…”
  • Right: “…students would talk more.”
  • Expect: “If schools would ban phones, …”
  • Repair: “No would after if.” Wait.

Open Lesson Games “Second Conditional Auction”.
