SEGMENT 1 — GRAMMAR: DESPITE / ALTHOUGH (~16 min, 20:14–20:30)

1A · Present the difference (6 min, 20:14–20:20)

Present. Type two sentences in the chat and ask what comes after each linker:

Although it was late, she kept working. → a clause (subject + verb)

Despite the late hour, she kept working. / Despite being tired, she kept working. → a noun or -ing

Rule, word for word: “although + sentence; despite + thing or -ing. Never despite of.”

  • ⚠ TRAP (despite + clause)
  • Spring: “Join them with despite: It was raining. We went out.”
  • Right: “Despite the rain, we went out.”
  • Also right: “Despite it raining, we went out.”
  • Expect: “Despite it was raining, we went out.”
  • Repair: “What comes after despite: a sentence or a thing?” Wait.
  • After a miss: she writes both versions, the although one and the despite one, under each other.
